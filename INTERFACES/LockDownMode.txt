The "Lockdown Mode" Interface
The interface is visually organized into three principal areas: the status area, the navigation/header area, and the content area:
•	Status Bar (Top Tier): Spanning the uppermost portion of the display from left to right:
o	Time: Displays the local time as 21:39.
o	Dynamic Island: The Dynamic Island is centered at the top of the display. A solid red circular indicator is visible toward its left side, consistent with the iOS screen-recording status indicator.
o	Connectivity & Power: The Airplane Mode icon denotes that wireless network transmissions are currently disabled. The adjacent battery icon is completely filled, displaying the numeric charge level 100 centered within the housing.
•	Navigation & Header Bar (Middle Tier): Establishing the user's hierarchical location within the iOS environment:
o	Back-Navigation: The upper-left margin displays a back-navigation link labeled "Back" accompanied by a directional chevron.
o	Primary Heading: Centrally aligned with the menu context, rendered in bold black typography: "Lockdown Mode".
•	Interface Body (Lower Tier):
o	Introductory Module: Positioned at the upper portion of the viewport immediately below the navigation bar, presenting an introductory visual block composed of the following elements:
	Icon Graphic: A solid blue rounded square containing a white palm hand symbol.
	Primary Heading: Centrally aligned or positioned directly adjacent, rendered in bold typography: "Lockdown Mode".
	Descriptive Text: Sub-text detailing the function of the interface: "Turn on this extreme protection if you believe you're being targeted in a cyberattack. App, website, and feature functionality will be limited and some experiences may be completely unavailable."
	Learn more...: Displayed in blue-colored text suggesting a hyperlink.
	Turn Off Lockdown Mode: Visually separated within the module by a dividing line, displayed in red-colored letters as an interactive control.
o	Protection Explanations Module: A listing detailing the functional changes that take place when the device is set to this mode:
	Messages: Most message attachments are blocked, and some features are unavailable.
	FaceTime: Incoming FaceTime calls from people you have not previously called are blocked. Features such as SharePlay and Live Photos are unavailable.
	Web Browsing: Some web technologies and browsing features are blocked.
	Photos: Location will be excluded by default when sharing photos, and Shared Albums will be removed from Photos.
	Device Connections: Wired connections with another device or accessory while your iPhone is locked are blocked.
	Wireless Connectivity: Automatically joining non-security Wi-Fi networks and 2G Cellular Connectivity are blocked.  
	Apple Services: Incoming invitations for Apple Services from people you have not previously invited are blocked.  
	Profiles: Configuration profiles, such as profiles for school or work, cannot be installed.  
o	Additional Controls:
	Configured Web Browsing: Displayed as an active menu item.
	2G Cellular Connectivity: Toggled Off (gray).
•	Text under this setting: For enhanced security, 2G cellular connectivity is turned off when in Lockdown Mode. Emergency calls may temporarily turn on 2G, if needed.
